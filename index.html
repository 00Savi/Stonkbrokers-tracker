Network Analytics

This dashboard maintains an active ledger of the ecosystem by directly indexing and replaying all historical logs from the StonkBrokers Activation Manager smart contract.

When a broker activates, they lock tokens from the circulating supply to secure their weighted multiplier. Upon the execution of a true ownership transfer (NFT sale or wallet move), the smart contract automatically triggers an ActivationCleared event. This strips the tier, resets the multiplier back to base weight, and mathematically increases the yield share for all remaining active holders.
