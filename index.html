Methodology & Disclaimer

Yield & ROI (Global Network Oracle): Cash-on-Cash (CoC) returns are calculated dynamically based on the selected project's architecture. Automated distributions sample incoming transactions to an active oracle wallet. Soft-staking mechanics track real-time global, aggregate distributions flowing outbound from the central protocol vault. This total is mathematically divided against the live active network weight and evaluated against AMM floor entry costs.

Token Burn Tracking: The total burnt token count is calculated by directly indexing the balances of the network's standard dead addresses (0x000... and 0xdead), combined with the tokens permanently locked inside the protocol's Activation Manager/Staking Vault smart contracts. The equivalent metric divides this total by the standard token tier requirement.

Ecosystem Activation Metrics: Live network activation metrics are derived by parsing the entire historical event log of the protocol's Activation Manager smart contract. The script actively tallies 'Activated' and 'ActivationCleared' on-chain events to mathematically map the live network weight, chronological tier flow, and tier composition.

True Circulating Supply: Calculated as the Genesis Supply minus the live inventory currently held within the AMM Pool. Burnt/locked tokens are physically trapped inside the AMM Vault, meaning they are inherently excluded from the circulating supply.

Holder Metrics & Anti-Dust Filter: Unique token holders are calculated by directly indexing the blockchain ledger and applying a strict "Anti-Dust" filter. Any wallet holding less than 1 full token is discarded to eliminate MEV bots, DEX routers, and abandoned fractional dust. Furthermore, protocol system wallets (the AMM Vault and burn addresses) are explicitly removed from all holder counts to provide a mathematically honest representation of unique human ownership.

Disclaimer: Tracked yield values are calculated using Mark-to-Market spot pricing at the exact time of the dashboard's last automated sync, rather than the historical price at the time of the drop. Yields fluctuate based on network activation weight, market token prices, and community protocol volume. This is a community-built tracking tool and does not guarantee future returns.
